SimplyRemind Privacy Policy

Updated November 11, 2026. Applies to versions with iCloud Sync and bilingual feedback.

Task data and iCloud Sync

Tasks, completion states, and quick templates are stored on your device when iCloud Sync is off.

You can turn on optional iCloud Sync in Schedule → Settings. Task contents, reminder times, completion states, and quick templates are then synced to your current Apple Account’s private iCloud database through Apple CloudKit for syncing across devices and restoring data on a new device. No app account is required. The developer does not store task data on a separate server.

When offline, the app keeps local changes and retries syncing when a connection is available. Sync can take time. Other devices cancel local reminders after receiving completion or deletion changes. Completed records are kept for seven days. To prevent offline devices from restoring deleted tasks, sync retains deletion markers containing random record identifiers and version information, without deleted task contents.

Turning sync off keeps existing local and iCloud data and stops further syncing. Changing Apple Accounts pauses sync and keeps local data. The app does not automatically upload the previous account’s data to a new account.

Language preference

Your selected app language is saved on this device. It controls interface text and the feedback provider. English feedback opens Google Forms. Chinese feedback opens Tencent Survey.

Feedback

You may submit an issue or suggestion using the form. Any text, contact details, or attachments that you choose to submit are processed by the selected form provider and made available to the developer to address problems, reply to suggestions, and improve the app. Available fields, sign-in requirements, and attachment options depend on the form’s current configuration.

The app does not automatically pass reminder tasks, completion history, your Apple Account, or a unique device identifier to either form. It does not automatically fill in the app version or iOS version.

Google Forms is provided by Google. Google may process service information such as IP address, browser and device information, and activity when you access its services. If a form requests sign-in or an email address, review the requirements shown on that form. See Google’s Privacy Policy for details.

Tencent Survey processes form status information such as response start and submission times, duration, IP address and approximate region, device, and operating system for response validation, analytics, and feedback services. If you choose platform sign-in, login type and nickname may also be included. The platform may share response status information with the form owner. See the Tencent Survey Privacy Notice for details.

The embedded browser uses temporary website storage. The app does not persist new form drafts. Any provider-supported resume feature follows that provider’s settings. Unsent legacy email drafts remain on your device and can be copied using the feedback menu, then pasted into a form by you.

If you choose to upload an image or take a photo, the system may ask for the corresponding permission. The app does not scan or automatically upload your photo library.

The developer uses received feedback only to address issues, reply to suggestions, and improve the app, without advertising or user profiling. To request deletion of submitted feedback, contact alfred0918@163.com.

Notifications

Reminders use local notifications scheduled separately on each device. Enable notifications on your new device after restoring data. With iCloud Sync enabled, the app also uses silent remote notifications to learn about data changes. Delivery may be delayed or skipped, so the app also checks for changes when opened.

Tracking and advertising

The app’s native features do not include advertising or cross-app tracking, and the developer does not sell user data. Embedded third-party form pages follow their providers’ service and privacy practices.
